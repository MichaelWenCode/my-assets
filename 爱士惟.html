Solplanet, established in 2007, boasts over 15 years of experience as a prominent inverter manufacturer. In 2022, they secured the 9th position in global inverter shipments and held a top 5 spot in global residential inverter market share. Operating under the brand name 'AISWEI,' they clinched the top position in China's residential market with a remarkable 1/3 market share. Solplanet has established a strong presence in Europe through direct collaboration with the leading distributor IBC and is the largest inverter brand by revenue in Turkey. Furthermore, they have made significant strides in the Brazilian market. Their commitment to innovation is evident with 200+ patents worldwide, making it to the top 4 fastest-growing inverter company in the world YoY.
